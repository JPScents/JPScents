## Scope and authority

The architecture is **APPROVED**. Its 22 rules and decisions D1–D5 govern ownership, dependencies, transactions, data invariants, and required evidence. The 25 approved standards govern how agents write, organise, and verify code within those boundaries. The enforcement matrix identifies the checks already in place and the evidence that still depends on tests or review. Examples marked as target patterns illustrate approved behaviour that is still awaiting implementation. [Approved decisions](../architecture/ARCHITECTURE.md#approved-decisions) · [Implementation gaps](../architecture/ARCHITECTURE.md#implementation-and-enforcement-gaps) · [Project instructions](../../AGENTS.md#L13)

Apply these conventions to new and materially changed code. A full feature refactor follows the repository's broader scope rules. Report unrelated baseline gaps without expanding a focused change. Preserve framework filenames, existing public contracts, data compatibility, and the approved architecture. Any conflict or deviation goes through STD-025.

### Alignment with the architecture

- **STD-001–009 and STD-018** implement the architecture's ownership and dependency rules through naming, module organisation, type contracts, and reuse. STD-007 preserves transaction cohesion under ARC-10 even when a function exceeds a review threshold.
- **STD-010–013** apply ARC-01, ARC-03, ARC-08, and INV-01 to route composition, browser state, and the props exposed to the client.
- **STD-014–017** apply ARC-04–06, ARC-09–11, and INV-01–07 at implementation boundaries. D1 places checkout decoding in the action. D2 keeps Orders in charge of its transaction and Catalogue in charge of stock operations using that transaction. D3 and D4 retain their approved order history and inventory requirements.
- **STD-019–025** put VER-01–03 and GOV-01 into practice through outcome-based tests, documentation, and accurate verification reports. The two image exceptions apply solely to the image lint rule under STD-005. All architecture rules and the accessibility requirements of STD-012 still apply.

## Standards-to-enforcement matrix

## Approved decorative-image exceptions
